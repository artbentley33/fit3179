Australian Specialty Cheese

A visual exploration of Australia's dairy industry

A DATA STORY

From milk production to award-winning cheese.

Australia's cheese industry connects dairy production, consumer demand, international trade and local craftsmanship. This visual exploration examines how milk is used, where cheese is produced, how consumption and trade have developed, and which producers have gained recognition.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

One industry, many connections.

Australia's cheese industry can be understood through the relationship between dairy resources, production, consumption, international trade and producer recognition. Together, these perspectives provide a broader picture of how the industry operates and where its different strengths lie.
