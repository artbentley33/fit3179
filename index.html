Australian Specialty Cheese

The story of Australia's cheese industry

Introduction

Australia's cheese industry connects dairy farming, food production, domestic consumption, and global trade. Cheese is the largest user of Australia's milk supply, making it an important part of the country's dairy sector. Victoria leads milk production, supporting a major part of the industry's raw material supply.

Beyond production, Australia's cheese market includes different varieties, nutritional profiles, and international trading partners. This visualisation explores how cheese is produced, consumed, imported, and exported. It also examines the producers and states recognised for their achievements in cheese competitions.

Conclusion

Australia's cheese industry combines large-scale production with diverse products. Cheddar leads cheese production, while cottage cheese stands out for its lower fat content. Consumption remains high throughout the year, showing cheese's continued place in Australian diets.

International trade is strongly connected to Asia, with Japan standing out as Australia's largest cheese export market. New Zealand leads cheese imports. In competitions, Real Dairy Australia is the leading producer by medals, while NSW and Victoria dominate state awards.
