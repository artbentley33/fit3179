Australian Specialty Cheese

The story of Australia's cheese industry

Cheese is one of Australia's most
important dairy products. It uses
more milk than anything else and
exports billions each year.

This dashboard explores how cheese
is made, eaten, traded, and
celebrated across Australia,
from farm to award stage.

Award-Winning Craft

Australian cheese combines commodity
scale with award-winning craft.
Victoria dominates production,
while NSW producers win the most medals.

Exports flow to Asia, led by Japan.
Domestically, cheese remains a staple,
though consumption has softened
in recent years.
