Australian Specialty Cheese

The story of Australia's cheese industry

Cheese is one of Australia's most important dairy products. It uses more milk than anything else, exports billions of dollars each year, and supports thousands of jobs across the country. This dashboard explores how cheese is made, eaten, traded, and celebrated across Australia — from farm to award stage.

Award-Winning Craft

Australian cheese combines commodity scale with award-winning craft. Victoria dominates production, while NSW producers win the most medals. Exports flow to Asia, led by Japan, and domestically cheese remains a household staple — though consumption has softened in recent years.
